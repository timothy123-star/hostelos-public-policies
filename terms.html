HostelOS Terms of Service — Controlled Pre-launch Pilot

Pre-launch pilot draft · Last updated: 10 November 2026 · Prepared for Nova Residence (approval pending)

1. What this pilot is

HostelOS is being tested as a digital hostel booking, management and security system. Participation is limited to invited testers. The pilot exists to validate booking conversations, consent-based roommate matching, demonstration payment reconciliation, photograph review and QR credential workflows before any operational launch.

2. Information you submit

  • Submit only your own information, or information you are authorised to submit.
  • Do not submit another person’s photograph or personal details without that person’s permission. Photographs are reviewed by management and retained as a review history (see the Privacy Policy).
  • Keep test data realistic but minimal; do not submit real bank account details, as none are needed or wanted for the demonstration.

3. Demonstration payments are not real payments

Bank-transfer and reconciliation features in this pilot are synthetic demonstrations. No real money moves, HostelOS is not a payment gateway, and there is no live bank integration. Any “payment verified” state in the pilot reflects a manager testing the approval workflow against a simulated ledger — nothing more.

4. Not a tenancy or accommodation confirmation

Nothing in this pilot — including any booking, allocation, message or QR credential produced during testing — constitutes a tenancy agreement, lease, or official confirmation of accommodation. No priority, room right or financial obligation arises from pilot participation.

5. Acceptable use

  • Do not attempt to access manager functions, other testers’ records, or credentials issued to someone else.
  • Do not submit unlawful, offensive or misleading content through WhatsApp or upload links.
  • Single-use upload links and credentials are personal; do not share or reuse them.

6. Availability and changes

The pilot is provided for testing on an “as is” basis and may be suspended, reset or changed at any time without notice. Test infrastructure (including the temporary access tunnel) may change between sessions.

7. Approval of permanent terms

Permanent operational terms — including pricing, refunds, house rules, liability, official contacts and the appointed Data Protection Officer — must be defined and approved by Nova Residence before public resident onboarding. Until then, this page is the entire governing document for the pilot.
