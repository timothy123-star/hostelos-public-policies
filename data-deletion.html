Data Access & Deletion Requests — Pilot Process

Pre-launch pilot draft · Last updated: 10 November 2026 · Prepared for Nova Residence (approval pending)

1. How to make a request

  1. Email timothyokolo321@gmail.com.
  2. Subject line: HostelOS Data Access or Deletion Request.
  3. In the message include:
    • your full name (as registered in the pilot);
    • your registered WhatsApp number (in international format, e.g. +234…);
    • whether you are requesting access (a copy/confirmation), correction, or deletion of your pilot data, and which items this covers (for example booking record, photograph, conversation record).

This mailbox is a temporary pre-launch contact for testing and privacy/data-deletion requests. It is not Nova Residence’s appointed Data Protection Officer or permanent official contact.

2. Identity verification

To protect your data from disclosure to the wrong person, you will be asked to verify your identity before anything is shared or removed. Verification is kept reasonable and proportionate — typically a reply from, or confirmation via, the registered WhatsApp number, and matching of the name and booking details you provide against the pilot record. If verification fails, the request cannot be actioned and you will be told what else to provide.

3. How requests are handled

  • Requests are handled manually by the pilot team. The aim is to acknowledge receipt within 7 days and complete straightforward requests within 30 days of successful verification.
  • Access requests are answered with a description of the pilot data held about you (for example resident record, booking/allocation state, payment reference status, photograph review status, credential status) rather than with database exports.
  • Deletion, where it applies, removes your pilot data from the live application records (resident, booking, conversation, photograph and credential entries linked to you). There is currently no automated or instant self-service erasure in the application.
  • You will receive a written confirmation of what was done.

4. Where deletion may be limited

Some data may have to be retained where a lawful obligation requires it — for example audit-log entries recording manager decisions, or payment reconciliation records that must be kept for accountability. In such cases only the minimum required data is retained, it is not used for any other purpose, and the reason will be explained in the response.

5. Complaints

If you are dissatisfied with the handling of your request, reply to the same address explaining why. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. The permanent complaints and DPO channel for Nova Residence is to be confirmed on approval of these policies.
